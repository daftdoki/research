Ada Lovelace translated Luigi Menabrea's paper on [[analytical-engine|Babbage's Analytical Engine]]
and added a set of notes longer than the paper itself.[^note-g]
Note G sets out, step by step, how the Engine could compute Bernoulli numbers.[^note-g]

Whether this makes her "the first programmer" is debated; see [[talk/ada-lovelace]]. [synthesis]
