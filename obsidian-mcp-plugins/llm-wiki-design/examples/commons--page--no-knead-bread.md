1. Mix 430 g flour, 1/4 tsp instant yeast, 8 g salt and 345 g water; cover.[^nyt-2006]
2. Leave 12-18 hours at room temperature until bubbly.[^nyt-2006]
3. Shape, rest 1-2 hours, bake in a preheated covered pot at 230-260 °C. [cn]

[^nyt-2006]: Bittman, NYT 2006, quoting Jim Lahey. Quantities converted from cups; check.
